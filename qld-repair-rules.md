# QLD repair notes (seed context, from the RTA)

**Checked against the RTA website on 2026-10-08.** Sources: [Emergency repairs](https://www.rta.qld.gov.au/during-a-tenancy/repairs/emergency-repairs), [Entry to the property](https://www.rta.qld.gov.au/during-a-tenancy/living-in-the-property/entry-to-the-property). General information only. The triage agent treats these as background, never as advice, and puts borderline cases under "things to confirm". It never tells a tenant what the law says.

## Emergency repairs (RTA list)

- a burst water service or a serious water service leak
- a blocked or broken toilet
- a serious roof leak
- a gas leak
- a dangerous electrical fault
- flooding or serious flood damage
- serious storm, fire or impact damage
- a failure or breakdown of the gas, electricity or water supply
- a failure or breakdown of an essential service or appliance for hot water, cooking or heating
- a fault or damage that makes the premises unsafe or insecure (including smoke alarms)
- a fault or damage likely to injure a person, damage property or unduly inconvenience a tenant
- a serious fault in a staircase, lift or other common area that unduly inconveniences the tenant's access or use
- repairs needed to meet minimum housing standards

Anything not on this list is a routine repair.

## When the agent can't be reached

- The tenant first tries the emergency contact or nominated repairer on the tenancy agreement.
- If they can't be reached within a reasonable time, the tenant or property manager can arrange repairs **up to a maximum of 4 weeks' rent**.
- A tenant who pays asks for reimbursement in writing with receipts, and allows **at least 7 days**.

## Routine repairs and entry

- Entry for repairs or maintenance needs **48 hours' notice** (entry notice, Form 9).
- Entry must be **between 8am and 6pm, Monday to Saturday**, unless the tenant agrees otherwise.

## How the app uses this

- Harbourline's own Emergency / Urgent / Routine categories drive the board.
- Harbourline's Emergency category follows this list for hot water and cooking (changed 2026-10-08). Other borderline cases go under things to confirm.
